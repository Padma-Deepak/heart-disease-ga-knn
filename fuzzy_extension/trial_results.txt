CONCLUSION
GA-Fuzzy-KNN improves mean accuracy over fixed KNN by 0.54 percentage points, but is 1.03 percentage points below the existing GA-KNN. Its mean ROC-AUC is marginally higher (0.9238 versus 0.9223). It does not improve the existing best mean accuracy or F1.

GA-Fuzzy-KNN versus existing GA-KNN: 1 win, 3 ties, 6 losses.
GA-Fuzzy-KNN versus fixed KNN: 6 wins, 1 tie, 3 losses.

The exhaustive fuzzy grid control achieved 85.98% mean accuracy, also below GA-KNN. All GA choices matched the best inner-CV score, but some tied configurations differed from grid search and produced different held-out predictions. No test result was used to choose between tied configurations.

METHOD
Exploratory extension using the exact original ten outer splits, already inspected in earlier work. StandardScaler and training fuzzy memberships are refitted inside every inner-CV training fold. Initialization k=5 excluding self, U=.51*onehot(y)+.49*neighbour frequencies. Query weighting d**(-2/(m-1)), normalized in log space, zero-distance memberships averaged. k=1..51, three distances, fuzziness in [1.5,2,2.5,3,4]; fixed fuzzy baseline k5/Euclidean/m2. GA pop50/gen100/tournament3/crossover.8/mutation.1/one elite. Grid control spans all 765 candidates and never changes GA choice. Training-fold distance geometry is reused to accelerate scoring; verified against independent pipeline CV for two predetermined configurations on every split. Hard class ties favour 0. Same numeric categories and retained zeros as the original experiment. Not a 2025 paper replication and not a fresh confirmatory test.

Membership scores are fuzzy degrees, not calibrated clinical probabilities. This fuzzy method extends the earlier project; it is not a fuzzy method reported in the selected 2025 paper.
